# Acknowledgements

Write Like Me's named-pattern audit and minimum-effective-edit emphasis were strengthened after reviewing Peter Yang's MIT-licensed [No AI Slop](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop) skill.

We learned from its clear detect-only workflow, contextual voice preservation, and concrete naming of weak writing patterns. We implemented those lessons independently in Write Like Me's own architecture and did not adopt blanket vocabulary bans.

Write Like Me extends the category with evidence-aware personal profiles, provenance and reliability controls, dictated-answer handling, portable correction learning, untrusted-sample isolation, and deterministic rewrite verification.

Thank you to Peter Yang for open-sourcing his work and making the wider writing-tools category better.

No endorsement, partnership, or collaboration by Peter Yang is implied.

## Distribution and language-variety inspiration

Deepak Ganapathi's independently developed MIT-licensed [Write Like Me](https://github.com/DeepakGanapathi97/write-like-me) project demonstrated a particularly simple multi-agent installation flow and called useful attention to preserving Indian English while removing generic AI-writing habits.

Those ideas prompted us to improve our distribution and make language-variety protection explicit. Our installer and language-variety contract were implemented independently: the installer adds modified-file protection, timestamped restore points, path validation and no raw-sample storage, while the language contract requires evidence and forbids identity-based dialect performance.

The projects share a name and broad category but have separate authors, repositories and architectures. No endorsement, partnership or collaboration by Deepak Ganapathi is implied.
