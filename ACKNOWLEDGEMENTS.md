# Acknowledgements

Write Like Me's named-pattern audit and minimum-effective-edit emphasis were strengthened after reviewing Peter Yang's MIT-licensed [No AI Slop](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop) skill.

We learned from its clear detect-only workflow, contextual voice preservation, and concrete naming of weak writing patterns. We implemented those lessons independently in Write Like Me's own architecture and did not adopt blanket vocabulary bans.

Write Like Me extends the category with evidence-aware personal profiles, provenance and reliability controls, dictated-answer handling, portable correction learning, untrusted-sample isolation, and deterministic rewrite verification.

Thank you to Peter Yang for open-sourcing his work and making the wider writing-tools category better.
