# Community code of conduct

Write Like Me welcomes careful disagreement, bug reports, synthetic examples
and contributions from people with different writing traditions and technical
backgrounds.

Participants must be respectful, specific and considerate. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, deliberate humiliation and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

This project deals with personal writing. Never post somebody else's draft,
profile, correspondence or identifying information without their explicit
permission. Prefer synthetic or thoroughly redacted examples.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules and
may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. For an
ordinary concern, open an issue without private material. For a sensitive
security or privacy concern, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
